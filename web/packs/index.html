Sound packs

Eight more pieces at a time. A pack sits on the FM-1 beside AISLING's own eight: turn PRESET past the eighth piece and you are in it. Hear one here first; put it on over USB; take it off again when you want another.

No FM-1 connected. You can listen without one.

Loading the packs.

Listening plays the same code the FM-1 runs, in this page. Installing needs Chrome or Edge, a USB cable, and the FM-1 stopped (press PLAY and let the sound die away). The FM-1 holds one pack at a time; your own changes to a pack's pieces go when the pack does, so back them up first if you want them.

A pack needs AISLING or later. Update the FM-1 if this page says it is older.
