Sound packs

Eight more pieces at a time. A pack sits on the FM-1 beside AISLING's own eight: turn PRESET past the eighth piece and you are in it. Hear one here first; put it on over USB; take it off again when you want another. Connect your FM-1 to see every piece on it, and to add or remove the pack's pieces one at a time.

    Add a pack from a file

    Have a pack someone made or sent you? Choose its .aispack file, or drop it here. It is checked, then kept in this browser beside the packs above, to hear and to install. A pack can come by a link too: "Copy a link to it" on any pack of yours makes one to pass on.

    Listening plays the same code the FM-1 runs, in this page. Installing needs Chrome or Edge, a USB cable, and the FM-1 stopped (press PLAY and let the sound die away). The FM-1 holds one pack at a time: eight places beside its own eight. A pack's piece can carry sounds of its own (a Patch for a layer): such a pack needs AISLING 1.6, and the page says so if your FM-1's is older. Adding or removing a piece makes a new pack of the eight in this page and installs it; the pieces that stay keep what you made of them. When a pack leaves it, this page first keeps its pieces as you left them, in this browser, and offers them back when that pack goes on again; save them to a file to carry them elsewhere.

    A pack needs AISLING or later. Update the FM-1 if this page says it is older.
